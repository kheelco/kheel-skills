# Reviewing a job domain analysis

Report findings, not fixes, and offer fixes as proposals. For a small change, check what changed and every job it
names.

1. **Structure.**
   - Jobs named that are not in the topology (a new job is proposed to the topology, not defined here).
   - Sub-jobs whose executor differs from their higher job's; sub-jobs with no statement, objective or completion.
   - Episodes with no trigger, immediate result or completion; episodes not attached to a job.
   - Relationships with no kind, a kind outside the list, or linked to a job broader than the one they bear on.
   - Open classifications from the topology neither settled nor recorded as open with evidence.
2. **Reasoning.**
   - Identity concluded from a shared objective, the same executor, a different trigger or source, or the stage test
     alone, without the other tests.
   - A sub-job denied identity because it is part of something larger, or every episode made a sub-job.
   - A classification chosen for a tidier map.
   - Changing the world folded into recording it; a seam stated one way where recording gives the change effect, or a
     legal effect asserted without its source checked.
   - Obligations placed without the outcome question answered; a broader job's obligation listed as adjacent.
   - Boundaries not tested against an exceptional case, a different setting or a change of technology or regulation.
3. **Against the rest.**
   - Topology changes the analysis implies but has not proposed.
   - Job maps whose decomposition disagrees with the analysis.
   - Words in jobs, episodes and cases that the glossary does not hold.
