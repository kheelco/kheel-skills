# Reviewing a job domain analysis

Report findings, not fixes, and offer fixes as proposals. For a small change, check what changed and every job it
names.

1. **Structure.**
   - Jobs named that are not in the topology (a new job is proposed to the topology, not defined here).
   - Relationships with no kind, a kind outside the list, or linked to a job broader than the one they bear on.
   - Open boundary questions from the topology neither settled nor left open with evidence.
   - Steps, episodes or sub-jobs of a job classified here without its map (internal structure is the job map's work).
2. **Reasoning.**
   - Identity concluded from a shared objective, the same executor, a different trigger, source or success criterion
     alone, without the five tests.
   - Executors merged because they stand to a job the same way, though their desired outcomes differ.
   - A classification chosen for a tidier model or map.
   - Changing the world folded into recording it; a seam stated one way where recording gives the change effect, or a
     legal effect asserted without its source checked.
   - Obligations placed without the outcome question answered; a broader job's obligation listed as related.
   - Boundaries not tested against an exceptional case, a different setting or a change of technology or regulation.
3. **Against the rest.**
   - Topology changes the analysis implies but has not proposed.
   - Job maps whose resolutions disagree with the analysis's boundaries.
   - Words in jobs and cases that the glossary does not hold.
